## 0. The answer in five lines

1. **The system can already make its own trades, fast.** The Stage-0 harnesses replay public candles. MEASURED here: one `backtest_trend.py` run of DOGEUSDT at 1h (2020-08-01..2026-09-30, Binance Vision) took **5.7 s**. It emitted **1,421 trades** and **27,984 bar-states**. The candle fetch took 52 s, once per symbol. Binance Vision lists **900** USDT-M perp symbols (MEASURED from an S3 listing; the count includes delisted and short-history symbols). Live trades are not the bottleneck. Nobody has pointed the harnesses at the universe and kept the output as a training corpus.
2. **The counterfactual engine turns each trade into many labels.** One trade yields about 340 priced arms (design doc § 3.2), and every bar of a trade is a state. The existing corpus already holds **271,090** distinct trades (MEASURED, reproduced: 1,354,689 rows in 3,527 files under `comms/` + `research/`). That is **352,063** replay jobs (design doc).
3. **More rows are not the same as more information.** The trade-management models are scored on the money legs as they trade. Their test population stays 2,627 trades (the paths corpus). The first unit therefore asks whether broad-universe data **transfers** to those legs. It does not assume it.
4. **Bar-level labels are the largest source and the least useful one now.** The heads they feed are the ones the review paused. Only the vol head has a consumer that needs it.
5. **Desk labels made by Claude cannot scale.** Claude judges them in plan sessions. The scalable half is deterministic replay pricing, and that is source B.

**Why A sits above B.** B multiplies labels per trade, but its trade population is whatever A (and the existing corpus) supplies. A is also the only source that runs today with existing tools (MEASURED above). B needs `CF-B0-ENGINE-LIFT` first. Q-TM1 uses both: A supplies the trades and states, and B (or the TM-BENCH labeler, which is the same engine) prices the actions.

**What this does not claim.** 90k broad trades do not make 90k money-leg trades. On the money legs, the per-trade paired sd of policy − incumbent is ≈ 0.40–0.51 R (INFERRED, ML-PROGRAMME § 2.2). At 2,627 test trades the minimum detectable effect is ≈ 0.025 R/trade (2.80 × 0.45 / √2627). Broad data can lower a model's variance on the money legs. It cannot enlarge their test set. Unit 1 measures whether it helps at all.
