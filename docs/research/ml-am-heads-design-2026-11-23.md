## 0. The operator's frame (2026-11-23, row `AM-V2-CF-DIRECTED`, verbatim in the row)
Brackets move in both directions. We need to know where the momentum will stop (TARGET), when we are wrong (INVALIDATION), and to bank something on the way; a dip that recovers means sometimes the stop should be loosened to structure. No-TP and trail-only arms are diagnostic, never candidates (TP doctrine).

## 1. What the prior heads measured, and what is different now

**What is NOT different, and the design must carry the lesson:** (a) skill in a loss function did not pay in R, so skill is necessary and never sufficient here; the verdict is the arm's book-level R1-R4 reading. (b) The harness fills a stop at the stop price, which flatters a tighter stop (giveback doc § 4); arms that move a stop must be compared against an arm that moves the same number of stops (the random control, R4) and re-run gap-adjusted before any pass is believed. (c) 468k rows are not 468k independent observations: the replay jobs share timestamps and market paths across symbols, and the ETF-heavy leg list (`None|AAXJ` ... keys; whether `strategy: None` there is a data gap is UNVERIFIED) may dominate. The effective n is the number of independent time-clusters per leg and must be MEASURED in the census before a power statement is made.

**Honest prior (INFERRED):** the target head has a plausible path to value because the row note reports 72.8% of trend trades stopped after MFE >= 2R kept going (in-sample), which is a target-placement signal, not a skill-in-log-loss signal. The invalidation head is the riskier one: it is the same question the giveback predictor failed on, now with 400x the rows. Expect it to need the dip-recovers formulation to add anything.

## 2. The label table both heads need (a build, not training)
`scripts/research/cf_labels.py` (CF-D1-LABELS) emits **one row per trade**: `arm_net_r`, `delta_vs_replay`, `post_exit {verdict, r_diff, mfe_r, mae_r}`, `fidelity_ok`, `abs_gap_r`, `label_end` (MEASURED, the file header and `label_row`). It has **no per-bar path**, and the two heads act at every bar. So step one is a bar-expanded table built from the same cf_bulk shard records and the cf replay bars (`cf_bars.py` / `cf_core.py`; the shard's `cf.path` carries `horizon_end`; the per-bar fields available in the shard are UNVERIFIED and are the first thing the build unit reads):

For each graded trade with `fidelity_ok` and each bar k at which the replayed actual path is still open: `R0` = entry risk (entry to the original stop), `open_r_k`, `peak_r_k`, `mae_r_k`, and the forward labels below, all measured from bars after k to `label_end`. Rows whose forward window is truncated by the end of the data are **censored and excluded** (never filled), and the count is reported. Low-fidelity rows are kept in the table with their gap (as CF-D1 does) and excluded from the fit by default; a sensitivity arm fits on all rows.

## 3. Head 1: TARGET (where the move stops)
- **Label (per trade, per bar k):** `F_k` = maximum favourable excursion after bar k up to `label_end`, in units of `R0`, measured from the bar-k close (the "remaining" move), plus `P_j,k` = indicator that the path reaches `+j x R0` beyond the bar-k close before an invalidation level (section 4) is hit, j in {0.5, 1, 2, 3} (PROPOSED, fixed before the run).
- **Outputs:** quantiles of `F_k` at q in {0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9} (pinball loss, LightGBM quantile objective, monotone-rearranged) and `P_j,k` (log-loss, calibrated). The TP is then a prediction: place or move the target at the leg's chosen quantile (the level whose predicted reach probability equals the leg's registered reach rate, TP-doctrine reach calibration, `tp_prediction_derive.py`), extend it when the predicted quantile rises and pull it in when it falls (two-way).
- **Features, all point-in-time (bar k close is the last input):** entry-time state (the leg's own signal features, `R0` in ATR, the ml_entry score and side for ML legs, regime cell, entry hour); path state at k (`open_r_k`, `peak_r_k`, `bars_since_peak_k`, `mae_r_k`, bars open, realised vol ratio and ATR at k); structure at k from closed bars only (distance to the nearest prior swing high / low, range position, the 4h trend sign); market state at k (BTC or index return since entry, breadth, cross-sectional vol); funding. **Excluded:** every `arm_net_r`, `delta_vs_replay`, `post_exit` and `harness_net_r` field (hindsight by construction, CF-D1 header) and any bar after k.
- **Baseline to beat (PROPOSED, registered per leg):** (i) the leg's static bracket (`original_brackets`) and the hold policy (`replayed_actual`); (ii) for skill, the **unconditional per-leg quantile** of `F` (and the leg's empirical reach rate): a head that does not beat "use the leg's own average MFE" has learned nothing the TP doctrine does not already give; (iii) the best static alternative TP from the CF arms (`alt_sl*_tp*`), so a head that only rediscovers "wider TP is better in-sample" is caught.

## 4. Head 2: INVALIDATION (when we are wrong)
- **Label A, stop before target:** `S_k` = 1 if, from bar k, the path touches the invalidation level `L_k` (the leg's current stop, and a second family at the structure level, the nearest opposing swing) before it reaches the head-1 target level, else 0; right-censored rows excluded as above. Output `P(S_k)` per bar.
- **Label B, the dip that recovers:** `D_k` = 1 if within the next m bars the adverse excursion exceeds `a x R0` (a in {0.5, 0.75} of the way to the stop, PROPOSED) **and** the path then reaches the target before the original stop. These are the trades a tightened stop would have killed and hold would have won; the head's job is to tell `D` (loosen to structure, do not tighten) from `S` (tighten or exit). Label from the CF arm pair directly where it exists: `delta_vs_replay` of a tighter arm below zero and of a wider arm above zero on the same row is the decision-grade version of `D`, and is fit as a third **action-value** baseline (an arm chooser regressing `delta_vs_replay` per arm), which both heads' policies must beat.
- **Outputs:** `P(S_k)`, `P(D_k)`, and the implied action set: tighten (high `P(S)`, low `P(D)`), hold, or loosen to structure (high `P(D)`); banking (a partial at the head-1 target quantile) is a third action, tested as its own class.
- **Features:** as head 1, plus the adverse-excursion path at k (depth and bars of the current dip), volume and range expansion on the dip, and distance to structure from the stop side. Same exclusions.
- **Baselines:** the base rate of `S` / `D` per leg and bar-age; the giveback predictor's feature set refit on the same rows (so "400x the data" is separated from "better labels"); the action-value arm chooser above; hold.

## 5. Leakage, embargo, folds
- **Split unit is the trade, never the row.** All bars of one trade sit in one fold. Time-ordered folds with `purged_folds` (cf_labels.py): a training row whose `[entry, label_end]` window overlaps a test span plus the embargo is dropped; `embargo_s` >= the longest holding window of the legs in the fold (PROPOSED) and at least the 7-day + 25 h purge the ENTRY recipe uses.
- **Cross-sectional leakage:** rows at the same timestamps on different symbols share the market path; folds are cut by calendar time (not by symbol) and the week-clustered se of the side-fold report (`ml_decision_entry.py side-by-fold`) is the standard for any pooled mean.
- **Hold-out:** the most recent CF shards after the training end, claimed in the holdout ledger before any outcome in the window is read (`holdout_lock.claim_read`, deflated bar on re-reads, M13); features and every threshold (quantiles, j, a, m, action thresholds) fixed before the first fit; one family `AM-HEADS` for the ledger.
- **Train-only normalisation and hyper-parameters**, fixed LightGBM params registered up front, no per-leg tuning against the test folds.

## 6. Grading: model skill first, then the decision rule
1. **Skill (necessary, never sufficient), pre-registered per head:** target = pinball-loss skill vs the per-leg unconditional quantile and reach-probability calibration (reliability within +/- 0.05 on each j); invalidation = log-loss skill and AUC vs the base rate, with calibration, plus the `D` vs `S` separation. Both on purged out-of-fold rows, per leg and pooled, with the week-clustered se. A head failing skill stops here (no policy test).
2. **Decision (the verdict):** apply the head as a bracket policy on the CF replay path so it becomes an arm with a full net R path per trade, then grade it with **`RULE-AM-RISK-ADJUSTED-V1`**: R1-R4 (small P&L give-up, material drawdown or tail cut, fold majority, beats the matched random-intervention control of the same class), net of the full cost stack, on the whole book. **Limitation carried from the rule:** `BOOK-SIM-REAL-SIZER` does not exist yet, so `scripts/research/am_risk_adjusted.py` is a screen and never graduates a class except under the 2026-10-29 dated exception; the memo does not claim otherwise. Reported with every verdict: R giveback (mean, p95) and overall net P&L in % (never balances).
3. **Each action class is its own test:** target extend / pull-in, tighten, loosen-to-structure and bank are four units, each registered before its run; one failing says nothing about another.
