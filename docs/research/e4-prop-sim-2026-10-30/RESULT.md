## Read this before using the PASS

1. **The gate does not measure edge, and this shows it.** The simulator's own cost model gives the ledger a mean net R of **+0.0087 R per trade (full)** and **-0.0173 R per trade (365d)** on the current stack
   (+0.028 / +0.004 on the venue stack); the driver's cost gives +0.0088 / -0.0170, so the two agree. A leg with a NEGATIVE mean R on 365d still reads EV +$23..+$116 per life. The reason is structural: a purchased
   attempt costs $20-$120 and pays 80% of a 9-12% profit target ($360-$960) on a pass, and the registered ceilings (c = 1 - 2F/W) sit at 0.73-0.89 where break-even is 0.81-0.94, so any ledger that can pass the
   evaluation more than a small fraction of the time has positive EV. The seeds vary the resampling of one fixed ledger; they carry no uncertainty about the ledger's own mean.
2. **The absolute Stage-0 test FAILED** (RQ-20261030-e18g-03, same PR): held-out mean net R per trade +0.022 R, 95% weekly-cluster CI **[-0.072, +0.110]**, n 856. E4 is an edge over random entries (S3 +0.184 R, held-out PASS), not a
   demonstrated profit. **A gate PASS on a leg that fails Stage 0 is not a promotion case: E4 stays demo-only and is not a prop candidate.** The prop gate and Stage-0 answer different questions; the ladder needs both.
3. **Window.** 1.87 years, not 3; the 365d window is 90% DEV (the exploration sample), so it is not independent of the selection. Half the ledger (held-out) is out of sample.
4. **Not run (stated):** a six-symbol-leg book (the plan's unregistered diagnostic) and half-risk cells. E4 trades often (1,619 in 1.87 years across six symbols); the "leg busy" skip count is in each cell JSON (`mean_trades_skipped_leg_busy_per_life`).
5. **Proxy and fills:** Binance candles for Bybit, entry at least one 1h bar after the event, no live-fill modelling.
